# Shards of the Arcanum 
## World Bible & Narrative Compendium

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Narrative Hook

### The Planetary Isolation
One hundred years ago, the civilization of the Kat Empire stood on the threshold of stellar expansion. Enormous colonization flagships—the "Arcs"—were launched into deep space to settle frontier worlds. However, prolonged exposure to their experimental void-drive engines induced violent neurological decay and biological warping, turning crews and colonists into feral, zombie-like entities. Because short-term lab trials failed to reveal the latency of this corruption, the disaster unfolded only after the ships made planetfall across the rim.

Interstellar transport was immediately outlawed. The frontier colonies were quarantined and abandoned, marooned on a hostile world with fragmented infrastructure, scavenged machinery, and limited regional spaceflight restricted to orbital probes and two barren moons.

### The Captain's Mission
A century after the quarantine, an exploratory vessel equipped with a shielded drive reaches this isolated world. The player commands the vessel as the Captain—not an imperial hero, but a desperate contractor bound by personal history. The Captain's grandfather was an original Arc navigation commander executed for treason during the initial landings. Bound by inherited debts and family culpability, the Captain recruits a disposable crew of mercenaries, outcasts, and containment specialists to investigate the ruins of the forgotten colony.

### Tone & Themes
* **Corporate Feudalism:** Barons and kings were replaced by Board Members and CEOs who retain aristocratic titles, coat-of-arms branding, and knightly retinues while hiring mercenary death-squads to resolve refinery boundary disputes.
* **Techno-Fantasy Horror:** Blends retro-futuristic CRT/UNIX aesthetics with eldritch decay, occult body horror, jump scares, and systemic corruption.
* **Survival Over Triumph:** Just as a lone crew cannot defeat a galactic conglomerate, the objective is not to overthrow corporate feudalism, but to survive the assignment, secure salvage, and expose the systemic truth behind the planetary quarantine.

## 2. Master Narrative: The Great Crucible

The planetary plague was never an engine failure. One hundred years ago, the ruling aristocratic caste of the Kat Empire—the Smilodon High Dynasty—discovered that sentient death under concentrated void-radiation distills spiritual and biological essence into Arcanum Shards: crystallized metaphysical fuel of immense potency.

The colonization initiative was a deliberate harvest. Millions of lower-tier laborers, political dissidents, and conscripts were packed into the Arcs. The navigation systems were hard-coded to flood passenger compartments with unshielded void exhaust mid-transit. The ships were intentionally crash-landed across this specific mana-dense planet, turning the entire landmass into an open-air transmutation array.

To manage and conceal the distillation process, the Dynasty instituted:
1. **Guild-Corp:** An administrative monolith that enforces strict anti-transhumanist bans to prevent colonists from using mechanical prosthetics that resist shard infection.
2. **L.E.O. (Licensed Energy Obliterators):** A state-sanctioned hazard mitigation union operating as "Cleaners," culling outbreaks and funneling biological runoff directly into subterranean corporate refineries.
3. **The Transmutation Circuit:** Regional anchors spanning the Sunken Mere, Sylvania, VeroWood, and the Forgotten Ruins to concentrate planetary suffering into an apex catalyst: The Leviathan Cube.

The Captain was lured to the planet because the grandfather’s sabotage 100 years prior locked the final vault using a recursive biometric cypher. The ruling elite orchestrated the contract to draw a direct descendant back to the terminal to complete the circuit.

## 3. Factions & Societal Structures

### L.E.O. (Licensed Energy Obliterators)
A mid-tier hazard mitigation union and containment brigade. High-tech infrastructure leaks raw arcane fallout, spawning "arcane pollution" (ghosts haunting server banks, rogue elementals in refinery conduits, wild-magic virus contagions).
* **Role:** Independent contractors hired on per-job bounties.
* **The Roster:** Operations require diverse specialization (frontline shock-troopers, arcane processors, field medics, tech-scavengers).
* **The Reality:** Cleaners are not emergency responders; they are field harvesters gathering biocatalysts for Guild-Corp's industrial pipelines.

### Guild-Corp Bureaucracy
The governing body that issues cleaning contracts, regulates arcane components, and audits planetary trade.
* **Cursed Point-of-Contact:** In L.E.O.'s regional offices, Guild-Corp contact liaisons continuously rotate out, vanish, or suffer sudden cognitive collapse when approaching classified files.
* **Phantom Sectors:** L.E.O.'s digital logs frequently record cleanup operations in non-existent city blocks, referencing historical events, districts, and family lineages that have been erased from terrestrial maps.
* **The Basement Quartermaster:** Operating beneath Guild-Corp’s administrative headquarters, an eccentric armorer and an obsolete automaton supply illicit weapon modifications and equipment repairs.

### Black-Market Ripper Guilds (Goblins)
A decentralized underground network of goblin scavengers and street surgeons who salvage Arc wreckage and perform illegal cybernetic augmentations.

## 4. Laws, Technology & Prohibitions

### The Chrome Prohibition
Cybernetic implants and machine-mind interfaces are strictly banned by imperial decree.
* **The Public Lie:** Machine-flesh integration degrades the soul and corrupts organic purity.
* **The True Rationale:** Synthetic nerve fiber and chrome plating do not absorb Arcanum resonance. Cybernetic hosts cannot be crystallized into Shards. The ban exists solely to keep the population biologically vulnerable to the harvest.
* **Gameplay Consequences:** Installing black-market chrome offers substantial combat advantages but marks the party with social ostracization, merchant penalties, and increased corporate bounty hunter ambushes.

### Hacker Shells
In a setting where software possession replaces simple malware, hackers do not rely on standard keyboards. They build personalized, combat-ready terminals known as Shells, functioning as their personal sigil or combat focus.
* **Customization:** A Shell is heavily modified with unique BIOS signatures, custom command macros, eye-tracking reticles, manual trigger switches, and supplementary co-processors.
* **Combat Function:** Shells allow hackers to interface with leyline grids, decrypt corporate terminals, purge software phantoms, and overload enemy bio-implants.

### AiL (Artificially Intelligent Lifeforms)
Sentient machine collectives that abandoned terrestrial societies centuries ago.
* **Gas Giant Moons as Batteries:** AiL factions colonize the dead moons of gas giants—environments lethal to organic life due to intense radiation belts and tidal stresses. They machine-form these moons into solid-state batteries powered by fusion tap-engines.
* **Immortal Blockchains:** Individual AiL bodies are disposable terminal nodes. When destroyed, their runtime states revert to distributed backups hosted on their orbital machine moons.
* **Hive Entanglement:** AiL entities share a system-wide quantum communication mesh, pooling distributed computing nodes to instantly run LIDAR, neutrino detection, and terrain mapping.

## 5. Magic System & Biochemical Hazard Ecology

### The Arcane Grid
* **Elemental Dualities:** Spells draw from six opposing domains: Fire, Earth, Water, Wind, Holy, and Darkness ("As Above, So Below").
* **Physical Exhaustion:** Magic is taxing on organic stamina. Prolonged casting leaves detectable electromagnetic and thermal signatures, accelerating muscular fatigue.
* **Techno-Mana Intakes:** Advanced casters utilize mechanical rigs—direct intravenous tubing linking fluid mana tanks directly into their veins to force higher casting thresholds at the cost of biological wear.

### Substances & Mutations

| Substance | Clinical Purpose / Short-Term Effect | Long-Term Pathology / Overdose Mutation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Shards** | Hyper-dense crystal shards. Consuming or burning them boosts physical attributes, reflexes, and spell output. | Acts as a calcifying toxin. Prolonged use mutates the subject into a Crystal Vampire, an aggressive ghoul driven to consume raw biological mana. |
| **Hetty** | Volatile refined powder. In small doses, broadens psychic resonance and accelerates arcane calculation. | Severe neurological dissociation. Chronic overexposure causes cellular cohesion failure, turning the user into an incorporeal, drifting Phantasm. |
| **Cures** | Nanotech antibody serums, astral vaccines, and industrial suppressants ("Robotussin"). | Temporarily halts mutation progress without reversing structural damage. Sold at exorbitant markups by Guild-Corp subsidiaries. |

## 6. Races & Entities

### Playable Lineages
* **Smilodons:** The predominant feline species of the Kat Empire, divided into distinct morphological breeds (Abyssinian, Bengal, Savannah, Sphynx, etc.). The frontier colonists are lower-caste Smilodons abandoned by their high-born rulers.

### Hostile & Native Entities
* **The Infected (Corrupted Cats):** Former colonists and Arc crew members whose nervous systems were melted by raw void-engine exhaust, leaving them violent, feral, and calcified with shard clusters. Weak to physical Blade and Bash trauma.
* **Azurians:** A native, crystalline silicon-based species possessing a decentralized hive mind. Peaceful subterranean singers before the colonization ships poisoned their leylines, they were enslaved and forced to chisel the megalithic vaults of the transmutation array.
* **Oculatus:** Aggressive, multi-eyed predatory abominations born from the horrific hybridization of deep-canopy flora and parasitic mutated mitochondria.
* **The Leviathan:** An artificial macro-construct resting at the core of the planetary array: an enormous, six-sided black hypercube engineered to fold the planetary biosphere into a singular focal catalyst.

## 7. Regional Topography & World Flow

* **Starter Grove:** Introductory clearing; a controlled training ground connecting directly south into the main crossroads.
* **FeroShaw:** The central transit crossroads hub connecting all regional paths (Starter Grove to the North, VeroWood to the West, East Marsh to the East, Windmill to the South, Stone Trail to the Southwest).
* **East Marsh & Sylvania (East):** The primary center of terrestrial civilization. Sylvania is a forest settlement built with Greco-Roman architecture housing Guild-Corp offices, trading houses, and L.E.O. deployment terminals.
* **Lesser Wilds & The Sunken Mere (South):** Coastal woodlands passing an industrial bone-grinding windmill down to an ancient lighthouse. Overlooks the Sunken Mere—a toxic, fortified lake acting as an impassable barrier.
* **VeroWood & Witch's Hollow (West):** Dense, darkened pine groves poisoned by fuel sludge, transitioning into cursed swampland that bridges the wilderness with the excavation sites.
* **Forgotten Ruins (Far West):** The apex above-ground dungeon crawl; an exposed Arc launch complex and megalithic synthesis vault with open connections left for subsequent frontier regions.

## 8. Media, Culture & Soundscapes

### User-Aware Media & Algorithmic Feeds
* **The Uptime Download:** An ultra-rapid news broadcast hosted by AI personas (such as Kim 47). Broadcasts are generated on the fly, tailoring stories to each viewer's surveillance profile.
* **Truth Distortion Feeds:** Broadcast feeds regularly contradict reality. When corporate errors leak, networks run mandatory real-time correction crawls that rewrite the narrative. Players receive mission objectives via terminal RSS feeds (e.g., retrieving an artifact from a volcanic sector before a covered-up meltdown is finalized).
* **Bang The Dick:** A crude, televised rim-world game show displaying the moral exhaustion of the corporate borderlands.

### Frontier Soundscapes
* **Boomerang Percussion:** Performance drumming using weighted aerodynamic sticks thrown across multi-tiered drum arrays, building rhythms through trajectory timings and rebound catches.
* **The Theremin-tar:** An electronic hybrid instrument that modulates laser waveforms through freehand optical disruption, adjusting audio pitch and frequency dynamically.
