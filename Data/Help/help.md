# Movement
W / Up       step forward
S / Down     step back (hold to turn around)
A / Left     turn left
D / Right    turn right
Q / E        strafe left / right
/            interact with the tile in front of you

# Your Party
Click a portrait to open that character's sheet: gear, stats, skills and front / back row.
Slots 1-3 are the front row and can be hit in melee. Slots 4-6 are the back row.

# Battle
Every fighter has a turn meter. When it fills, they act: Attack, Defend, Spell, Skill or Item.
Speed fills the meter faster. Level and equipment raise your stats.

# Menu
Press Esc or click the menu button (top left) to pause, save, load, change settings or quit.
